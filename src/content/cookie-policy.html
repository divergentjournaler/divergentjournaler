Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used when you visit Divergent Journaler.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites or third-party services may store in your browser. Similar technologies can also help services remember settings or measure interactions.

Cookies on this website

We have not intentionally installed analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this website. We do not use Google Ads or a Pinterest tracking pixel, and visitors do not have website login accounts.

Simply clicking an ordinary link to Pinterest, YouTube, or another website takes you to that provider, which may then use cookies according to its own policies.

Third-party content

Our Google Drive Vault makes material available through Google Drive. Depending on how you access that content, Google may use cookies or similar technologies.

Some pages may include embedded Pinterest posts or YouTube videos. Unlike ordinary links, embedded content may contact the third-party provider when the page or embed loads. Those providers may set cookies or use similar technologies, including for functionality or measurement, according to their policies.

For details, see Google's cookie information (including YouTube) and Pinterest's Cookie Policy.

How to manage cookies

You can review, block, or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how third-party content works.

Updates and contact

We may revise this policy as the site's features change. Questions can be sent to dialogues@divergentjournaler.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
