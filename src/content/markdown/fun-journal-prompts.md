Using prompts is a fun and helpful way to inspire your journaling journey. The list below is for those who would like to use a text list either to print or save into a notes app.

#### Fun Journaling Prompts

1.  Your favorite day ever spent with someone you love.
2.  Write about something that is not expensive but means alot to you.
3.  Describe your favorite childhood hiding spot.
4.  Describe your idea treehouse or fort for you right now.
5.  Who is your favorite superhero? Why?
6.  Write the lyrics of your favorite song.
7.  Describe your perfect day spent alone or with your favorite person.
8.  Who is your favorite actor? Why?
9.  Who is your favorite band? Why?
10.  Describe how you would spend an hour with your favorite singer.
11.  Make a list of your top 10 favorite things.
12.  If you had a time machine.. where would you go?
13.  Write about a silly or quirky habit that you have.
14.  If you could be invisble for a day... where would you go?
15.  Make a list of songs that make you feel happy or feel like dancing.
16.  Who have you been talking to the most lately? Why?
17.  What's something you'd like to get better at?
18.  What food do you wish you had right now?
19.  What movie could you watch over and over?
20.  What is the last thing that you ate?
21.  What was your favorite class in school?
22.  Describe the most comfortable piece of clothing you have.
23.  What hobby would you like to learn?
24.  Describe the last video you saw that made you laugh.
25.  Describe the last meme you saw that made you laugh.
26.  Describe your favorite book.
27.  What do you do to rewind and relax?
28.  Describe three places you would like to go.
29.  What is your favorite word and why?
30.  What is your favorite number and why?
31.  What is your favorite color and why?
32.  You find a hidden box; what's inside it?
33.  If your life was a movie; what would it be called?
34.  Describe the last concert you went to.
35.  What do you wish you were really good at?
36.  If you were an animal, what would it be?

I hope these prompts inspire you, break any creative blocks, or just give you a moment of fun and enjoyment with your journals! Click [here](https://www.pinterest.com/divergentjournaler) to visit my Pinterest and see all of these images and more.
