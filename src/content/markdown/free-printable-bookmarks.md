I have a love affair with bookmarks in all sizes and shapes, paper or metal; I just love bookmarks. Making free printable bookmarks allows me to save a bundle and keep this love affair moving right along!

Bookmarks serve as convenient tools for marking your place. They allow you easy access to any section in your journal. Because so many things happen in my journal simultaneously, I generally have four or five going at a time. Bookmarks also serve to help in studying, reading books, and more.

Below, you will see the bookmarks that I currently have available. Each will link to the printable PDF located on GDrive. If you would like to bypass downloading them individually, you can sign up for full access to the [Divergent Vault](https://divergentjournaler.com/divergent-vault/). The vault currently hosts over 100 free printables for your journaling needs. If you want to learn more before signing up, the [vault page](https://divergentjournaler.com/divergent-vault/) lists all available resources.

## Instructions

Each image of printable bookmarks is linked to the downloadable file on Google Drive. Once there, you can save it to your drive or download the file. When you print your file, set the scale to 100% so that you end up with four 2" x 6" bookmarks.
