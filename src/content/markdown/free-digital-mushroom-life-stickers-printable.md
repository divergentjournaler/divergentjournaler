## Mushroom Life Sticker Packs

Mushrooms, mushrooms, mushrooms! I love mushrooms and plants and all things nature. I have assembled this ready-to-print pack of 16 stickers for use in your journals, planners, or anywhere else. You can easily print them onto sticker paper or regular paper if you want to cut them out and use them. I am a regular paper person!

I have provided two types of stickers. The first is a plain sticker with no outer edge. The second is lined, so it will lay on the page with a slight shadow making it appear more sticker like. The purpose of this line is primarily for digital use. It helps to give the sticker a more real appearance. These stickers can be used in [Goodnotes](https://www.goodnotes.com) or any other journaling apps that accept PNG images. Using them at too large of a size will result in blurriness or pixelating.
