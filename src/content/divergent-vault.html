Welcome to a space where organization meets creativity. The Divergent Vault offers free journaling resources designed to help you stay organized on your journaling journey. You will find a range of printables (most of which can be used in Goodnotes as well) that include full planners, various planner components, stickers, bookmarks, and more. Most of these items were created for my personal use.
